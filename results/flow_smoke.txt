**v2 DRT verdict: did not converge within the 1.5 h bound**, same signature as
v1. The router produces no iteration artifacts through LibreLane's buffered
subprocess, so the failure is either extreme per-iteration runtime or a
livelock; it cannot be distinguished from outside.

Attempt 3 (bounded harder): DRT-only resume from step 43,
`DRT_OPT_ITERS=4`, 4 threads, `DRT_SAVE_DRC_REPORT_ITERS=1`, 08:09-09:30 UTC
(80 min). **No DRC produced**: step 46 log still 0 bytes, `drt-run-0/` empty,
no per-iteration report. Killed.

Attempt 4 (threading test): DRT-only resume from step 43,
`OPENROAD_THREADS=1`, `DRT_OPT_ITERS=4`, 09:30-10:15 UTC (45 min), killed.
**Also silent**: 101% CPU, step 47 log 0 bytes, `drt-run-0/` empty. The image
contains two OpenROAD builds, both the same binary
(`dcf36133a369abc8f3c5e5738cd4d82e4903c0e0`), and LibreLane has no
`OPENROAD_EXE`/`OPENROAD_BIN` override (it uses `openroad` from `PATH`), so
there was no alternative local binary to try.

**Conclusion (b): the local aarch64 OpenROAD/DRT runtime is the blocker**, not
density and not threading. Full attempt table and configurations:
`results/flow_report.md`. No local routed GDS exists for v1 or v2.

**Authoritative gate:** the Tiny Tapeout `gds.yaml` action on GitHub's x86_64
runners, not this aarch64 host. The identical DRT signature at 64.4% (v1) and
55.2% (v2, logic area) points at the local tool/runtime rather than the design
being unroutable; push the repo and use CI for the sign-off GDS, DRC/LVS and
slow-corner timing. Local area and post-global-route STA remain valid evidence.
